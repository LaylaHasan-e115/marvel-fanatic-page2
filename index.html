Overview

Marvel or, MCU, is a giant shared story world for superhero movies and television shows. It brings together marvel comics like Iron Man, Thor, and the Avengers, into the same connected universe.

How It Works

  • Started in 2008 with the movie Iron Man
  • Made by Marvel Studios
  • Uses a system of "Phases" to group stories together
  • Features seperate heros who meet up in big team-up films.
